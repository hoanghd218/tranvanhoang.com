# Product cover artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Covers are decorative visual metaphors; the detail hero, product tour and gallery use actual extension screenshots from the verified R2 library. No screenshot or interface was generated.

Final assets: `public/images/products/rocketflow-cover.webp`, `public/images/products/rocketlisting-cover.webp`, `public/images/products/rocketspy-cover.webp`. Each is a 1200 × 800 WebP derived from a 1536 × 1024 generated original, with editable product labels and local logos rendered separately by the website.

## Final prompts

### rocketflow

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: editorial 3D product cover for RocketFlow Chrome extension on Tony Hoang personal website, landscape 3:2.
Primary request: a refined sculptural metaphor for an AI image and video workflow. A horizontal arrangement of three translucent emerald glass modules connected by fine brushed gold rails, with a glowing natural-light glass core and one ivory ceramic frame resembling a film aperture, no interface.
Scene/backdrop: sunlit warm ivory architectural alcove, limestone ledge, subtle soft leaf shadow, generous calm empty space around sculpture.
Style/medium: premium photorealistic 3D editorial still life, elegant precision engineering, realistic material depth.
Composition/framing: medium wide eye-level three-quarter view, compact complete subject centered, keep important details in middle two-thirds, softly blurred cream background.
Lighting/mood: warm daylight, soft shadows, bright breathable scene.
Color palette: warm ivory #F5F3E9, emerald #1E765D, dark forest #123C32, restrained brushed gold #E4B445.
Materials/textures: clear emerald glass, matte ivory ceramic, brushed gold details, tiny chrome accents.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no UI screens, no product claims, no watermark, no people, no robots, no neon, no dark background. Decorative metaphor only.
